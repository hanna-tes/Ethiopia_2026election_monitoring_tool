How We Identify Weaponized Language

📚

1. Lexicon Engine

Curated dictionary of {{ total_terms|default:0 }} known ethnic slurs, violence incitement, and election interference terms in English, Amharic, and Oromo. Provides 100% precision for known high-risk keywords.

🤖

2. LLM Semantic Analysis

Advanced language model analyzes context and semantics to detect nuanced threats, coded language, and evolving slang that exact-match systems might miss.

🎯

3. AFRO-XLMR Transformer

Fine-tuned multilingual XLM-RoBERTa classifier specifically trained on Ethiopian languages (Amharic, Oromo, English). Detects 20 distinct categories of weaponized language in the Ethiopia election context.

AFRO-XLMR Detection Categories (20):

🔴 Violence 🔴 Inciteful 🔴 Call for Action 🔴 Dehumanization 🟠 Extremism 🟠 Ethnic Slur 🟠 Misogynistic 🟠 Slur 🟡 Derogatory 🟡 Inflammatory 🟡 Gender Disinformation 🟡 Stereotype 🔵 Ethnicity 🔵 Xenophobia 🔵 Religion 🔵 Homophobic ⚪ Ancestry ⚪ Class ⚪ Structural ⚪ Neutral

🔬 Methodology: Our hybrid approach combines rule-based precision with AI-powered contextual understanding. Each detection method votes on the classification, and we calculate a weighted confidence score to minimize false positives while catching sophisticated manipulation tactics. The AFRO-XLMR model was specifically fine-tuned on Ethiopian election discourse across Amharic, Oromo, and English to recognize these 20 distinct categories of harmful language with high accuracy.
